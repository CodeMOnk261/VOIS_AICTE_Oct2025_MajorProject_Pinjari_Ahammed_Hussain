# Netflix Dataset Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes the **Netflix dataset** containing over 7,000 titles (Movies & TV Shows).  
It explores content distribution trends, genre popularity, and country contributions to gain **strategic insights** into Netflix’s evolving catalog between **2008 and 2021**.

---

##  Dataset Description
The dataset contains **7,789 records** and **11 columns**, including:
- `show_id` — Unique identifier  
- `type` — Movie or TV Show  
- `title` — Title of the content  
- `director`, `cast` — Key contributors  
- `country` — Country of origin  
- `date_added`, `release_year` — When it was added / released  
- `rating` — Content rating  
- `duration` — Duration or seasons  
- `listed_in` — Genres or categories  
- `description` — Short description of the title  

---

##  Problem Statement
Netflix faces growing competition from global and regional OTT platforms.  
To stay ahead, it needs to **analyze its content trends** — by type, genre, and geography — to make **data-driven strategic decisions**.

> **Goal:** Understand *how Netflix’s content strategy evolved over the years* and recommend actions for better audience targeting.

---

##  Objectives
1. Analyze the distribution of **Movies vs. TV Shows** over time.  
2. Identify the **most common genres** and their changing popularity.  
3. Compare **country-wise contributions** to Netflix’s catalog.  
4. Provide **strategic recommendations** based on observed trends.

---

##  Key Insights (Sample)
-  Movies dominate Netflix’s catalog but TV Shows have steadily increased since 2015.  
-  Top genres include *International Movies, Dramas,* and *Comedies.*  
-  Major contributors: *United States, India, United Kingdom,* and *Japan.*  
-  Genre diversity has expanded globally after 2017, indicating international market focus.
